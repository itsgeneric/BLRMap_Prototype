2. **Author Self-Archiving & Preprints:** Author-accepted manuscripts can be accessed on institutional repositories (e.g., TU Munich for Winter [6], Microsoft Research for Delling et al. [8], and ResearchGate for Kotagi et al. [2] and Das & Maurya [3]).
3. **Browser Direct Download:** Papers `[1]` (*Promraksa et al.*) and `[4]` (*Fadilah et al.*) are Gold Open Access and can be downloaded immediately in any standard browser via their official DOI landing pages.
