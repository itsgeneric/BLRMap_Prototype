## 4. In-Depth Failure and Vulnerability Analysis

### 4.1 Empirical Failure of Test ROB-OD-07
- **Measured Result:** A $185\text{ m}$ Euclidean query between opposing sides of Mahatma Gandhi Road in the Central Business District resulted in a **$1,837.2\text{ m}$ ($1.84\text{ km}$)** route spanning 43 nodes and requiring $5.78\text{ ms}$.
- **Significance:** This failure demonstrates that the test assertion ($100\text{ m} < D < 500\text{ m}$) was naive to urban topography. MG Road features an unbroken concrete central median divider and strict paired one-way arterial flow (MG Road eastbound, Residency Road westbound). Navigating between opposing sides requires a complete circumnavigation of the urban block. 
- **Algorithmic Correctness:** The engine correctly refused to generate illegal U-turns across the concrete median or reverse through one-way streets, preserving physical network realism.

### 4.2 Circular Loop Generation on Identical OD Queries (`ROB-OD-01`)
- **Vulnerability:** When origin and destination coordinates are identical, [`two_wheeler_astar`](file:///C:/Users/comps/Desktop/Final Year Project/BLRMap_Prototype/routing/algorithms.py#L459-L605) returns `[start_node]` ($0.0\text{ m}$). However, [`build_split_route_candidates`](file:///C:/Users/comps/Desktop/Final Year Project/BLRMap_Prototype/routing/algorithms.py#L965-L1228) generates lateral offset waypoints that form closed circular out-and-back loops. Because `fallback_cap_length == 0.0`, the detour ratio defaults to $1.0$, and because the primary path has 0 edges, edge overlap is $0.0\%$. Consequently, 3 circular detour loops survive filtering.
- **Remediation:** Enforce a zero-distance guard at the top of [`build_split_route_candidates`](file:///C:/Users/comps/Desktop/Final Year Project/BLRMap_Prototype/routing/algorithms.py#L965).

### 4.3 Extraterritorial Coordinate Projection (`ROB-OD-06`)
- **Vulnerability:** Unconstrained $k$-d tree nearest-neighbor search in OSMnx projects coordinates located in the Bay of Bengal ($455\text{ km}$ east of Bengaluru) to perimeter boundary node `2247666159` without generating errors or warnings.
- **Remediation:** Enforce a strict geographic bounding-box validation check and a maximum snapping distance threshold ($r_{\max} \le 1,500\text{ m}$) at the API boundary before passing coordinates to the router.

## 5. Algorithmic and Operational Guarantees

1. **One-Way Directionality:** Confirmed across 31,391 strictly one-way edges in Bengaluru and isolated synthetic fixtures. The router never traverses directed edges in reverse ($0\%$ violation rate).
2. **Topological Funnels & Alternative Degradation:** In corridors bounded by lakes (OD_04) or expressway spines (OD_15), Perpendicular Offset cleanly rejects all 12 candidate legs and gracefully falls back to the primary route without crashing.
3. **Partition & Disconnection Handling:** When destinations are unreachable or cut off by bridge severance, the engine cleanly raises `nx.NetworkXNoPath` without infinite loops.
4. **Access Control Integrity:** Audit confirmed that 100% of restricted, private, and non-motorized ways were purged during graph preprocessing, ensuring two-wheeler routes remain exclusively on legal vehicular infrastructure.
