## 5. Identified and Resolved Edge Cases

During initial test suite authoring, two minor test-side discrepancies were identified and resolved:
1. **Coordinate Count vs. Node Count:**  
   In `test_02`, an initial assertion checked `len(coords) == len(route)`. However, `graph_manager.route_nodes_to_coords()` extracts intermediate Shapely `LineString` curvature points along OSM ways (resulting in 321 coordinates for 213 nodes). The assertion was corrected to `len(coords) >= len(route)`, reflecting the true geometry extraction logic.
2. **Positional Arguments for Split Route Candidates:**  
   In `test_04`, `build_split_route_candidates()` requires explicit geographic coordinates `(graph, graph_mgr, from_lat, from_lng, to_lat, to_lng, ...)`. The test harness was updated with the exact coordinate arguments, passing cleanly.
