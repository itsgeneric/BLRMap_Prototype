## Thematic Taxonomy Across Project Architectural Components

```
Audited & Verified Literature Survey Taxonomy (16 Verified Papers)
├── Pillar 1: Two-Wheeler Dynamics, Motorcycle Maneuverability & Route Choice
│   ├── [1] Promraksa et al. (2022) - Quantitative lane-filtering capacity & intersection queue physics
│   ├── [2] Kotagi et al. (2020) - Lateral clearance & road-width utilization in Indian mixed traffic
│   ├── [3] Das & Maurya (2019) - Microscopic filtering kinematics & car vs. motorcycle speed delta
│   ├── [4] Fadilah et al. (2024) - Motorcycle route choice behavior & narrow street accessibility
│   └── [5] Asvial et al. (2020) - Motorcycle-specific entropy-balanced routing in congested Asian cities
├── Pillar 2: Turn-Aware Shortest-Path Routing & Edge-Based Graph Representation
│   ├── [6] Winter (2002) - Edge-to-edge turn cost modeling & state-space dual graph expansion
│   └── [7] Geisberger & Vetter (2011) - Algorithmic routing in road networks with turn costs & restrictions
├── Pillar 3: Customizable Cost Functions, Multi-Criteria A* & Alternative Route Diversity
│   ├── [8] Delling et al. (2017) - Customizable Route Planning (CRP) with arbitrary cost functions & road penalties
│   ├── [9] Abraham et al. (2013) - Alternative routes, route diversity, and via-node penalty heuristics
│   └── [10] Bast et al. (2016) - Comprehensive survey of route planning speed-up techniques & A* heuristics
├── Pillar 4: Spatial Clustering & Recurrent Congestion Corridor Detection
│   ├── [11] Lee et al. (2007) - TRACLUS partition-and-group trajectory clustering (DBSCAN road-segment adaptation)
│   ├── [12] Du et al. (2020) - Spatio-temporal clustering to isolate recurrent diurnal congestion corridors
│   └── [13] Abbas et al. (2020) - Distributed streaming graph analytics for real-time edge congestion updates
└── Pillar 5: OpenStreetMap Modeling, Local Traffic Context & Benchmarking
    ├── [14] Boeing (2017) - OSMnx urban street network graph extraction & attribute preservation
    ├── [15] Luxen & Vetter (2011) - Real-time vehicle profile routing on OpenStreetMap graphs (OSRM)
    └── [16] Kumar et al. (2025) - Real-time Bengaluru traffic congestion forecasting & research gap benchmark
```
