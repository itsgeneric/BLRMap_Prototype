### 2.2 Section II: Related Work
- `[5] kirby1969minimum` — **Kirby (1969)**, *Operational Research Quarterly*, DOI: [10.2307/3008764](https://doi.org/10.2307/3008764).  
  *Manuscript Role:* Foundational pioneer of turn prohibitions and turn penalties in network routing algorithms.
- `[6] winter2002modeling` — **Winter (2002)**, *International Journal of Geographical Information Science*, DOI: [10.1080/13658810210148715](https://doi.org/10.1080/13658810210148715).

- `[7] geisberger2011efficient` — **Geisberger & Vetter (2011)**, *ESA 2011*, DOI: [10.1007/978-3-642-23719-5_28](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-23719-5_28).  
  *Manuscript Role:* Analyzes turn costs in Contraction Hierarchies, establishing that hierarchical speedups require static metric pre-computation.
- `[8] delling2017customizable` — **Delling et al. (2017)**, *INFORMS Journal on Computing*, DOI: [10.1287/ijoc.2016.0727](https://doi.org/10.1287/ijoc.2016.0727).  
  *Manuscript Role:* Reviews metric customization and turn penalties in modern road networks.
- `[9] bast2016route` — **Bast et al. (2016)**, *Algorithm Engineering: Selected Results and Surveys*, DOI: [10.1007/978-3-319-49487-6_2](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-49487-6_2).

- `[10] fadilah2024identifying` — **Fadilah et al. (2024)**, *Transportation Research Interdisciplinary Perspectives*, DOI: [10.1016/j.trip.2024.101147](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trip.2024.101147).  
  *Manuscript Role:* Documents empirical motorcycle route-choice behavior and cut-through navigation through residential street networks.
- `[11] asvial2020entropy` — **Asvial et al. (2020)**, *IEEE Access*, DOI: [10.1109/ACCESS.2020.3040375](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2020.3040375).

- `[12] zografos2004heuristic` — **Zografos & Androutsopoulos (2004)**, *Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies*, DOI: [10.1016/j.trc.2004.07.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2004.07.006).  
  *Manuscript Role:* Foundational heuristic framework for multi-criteria shortest path routing in transportation networks.

- **Allocation Decision:** **EXCLUDED FROM PAPER 1; RESERVED EXCLUSIVELY FOR PAPER 2.**

It will serve as a foundational citation for **Paper 2**.
